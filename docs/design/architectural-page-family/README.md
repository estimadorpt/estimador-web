## What to carry forward

Maintain the current approved logo and fonts; these mockups omit the exact symbol rather than redesigning it. Human activities and three-quarter views should replace bland profile silhouettes. Repeat architecture through drawing proportions, shallow depth, palette and line weight, not by placing the same house everywhere. Population may use windows and balconies; economy a shop shutter or awning fragment; football a small stand/goal entrance; elections a civic doorway or a few assembly-seat shapes. All serious pages can share the style without sharing the literal object.

Pine and paper remain foundational. Explore mineral blue, sage and limited clay in art only; this is not a chart/club/party palette migration. Decorations remain outside charts. No continuous animation; consider small user-triggered disclosure transitions, stable atlas selections and reduced-motion equivalents where appropriate. Research exports and dense tables can be entirely illustration-free.

## Prototype limitations

These are not implementation-ready pages or audited content. Do not copy generated claims about representativeness, privacy, available simulations or data access. Synthetic data do not automatically guarantee privacy or validity. The research chart contains schematic bars with no quantities, not real results or a finished chart design. Household categories need a valid mutually exclusive statistical definition before use. Generated maps are not authoritative boundaries. Controls, routes, search, people and illustrations are conceptual. Browser/mobile behavior has not been built or tested. The population image repeats figures in cards and is more illustrative than a final compact landing page may need; choose the useful entrances rather than copying every decorative object.
