Use case: logo-brand. Create a professional exploratory LOGO CONTACT SHEET for "estimador", a Portuguese independent data publication covering population, economics, elections and sport. The client rejected generic waves, pie charts, tiled circles and bracket-with-dot logos. This new round must demonstrate original typographic and conceptual thinking. SIX clearly different ideas on one landscape sheet in an immaculate 3-column by 2-row grid, evenly spaced with generous margins. Every cell has ONE large dark pine logo on FLAT ivory #F5F2E8, a small standalone icon beside it, and a tiny numeric label 01 through 06. All logos read exactly "estimador" lowercase. No website mockups, no slogans, no palettes, no decorative graphs, no glow, no shadows, no gradients, no fake stationery, no textures. Flat sharp vector-like design. Black-and-white clarity, dark pine #183F36 ink; a tiny butter yellow accent allowed in only two cells. Make the name itself memorable, not a stock font next to an arbitrary icon.
01: bespoke humanist wordmark with a distinctive lowercase e made by a single thick folded strip; subtle notch suggesting a measuring reference, no wave or globe. Compact striking mark, open counter, sophisticated.
02: typographic ligature identity. Craft the adjacent 'ti' within estimador into one elegant shared-stem ligature, with a strong asymmetric crossbar and a square dot. Rest of the word bespoke warm contemporary serif with sturdy strokes. Extract that ligature as an original monogram. Not a cross symbol.
03: an ingenious chunky lowercase e constructed from three unequal horizontal strokes joined by a curved spine; one stroke extends beyond an implied expected boundary like a measured estimate. Confident sculptural ink silhouette. Match with restrained custom grotesk lettering, distinctly different from 01.
04: a pure custom serif wordmark with a remarkable open 'o': a tiny deliberate gap at its upper right, ending in a squared terminal. A precise filled dot sits just beyond the gap like a observation completing a picture. Extract the open o plus dot as a beautifully balanced small mark; avoid eye, magnifying glass, location pin or power-button resemblance. Editorial, cultural, unexpected.
05: a single abstract interlocking 'es' monogram, carved in negative space from a tall soft-edged rectangular slab, pairing with a compact narrow lowercase wordmark. Bold Portuguese modernist publishing sensibility, not tech or luxury fashion. Easily drawable silhouette and strong 16px icon.
06: a custom friendly italic serif wordmark, forward leaning but calm, with an extended 'r' terminal and clever connected 'st' letter pair; use the stylized 'st' as a distinctive stamp. No separate unrelated symbol. Warm, credible and curious, no handwritten script.
All six are genuine alternative logo studies, not colour variants. Refine proportions, optical spacing, rhythm and negative space. Emphasize designer-quality simplicity and surprising letterforms. The final sheet must be completely legible and suitable for a real logo critique.

Finishing edit: replace transparency with solid opaque ivory, flatten all marks to dark pine, and correct the final r in options 04 and 06. Built-in image-generation tool used for generation and edit.
