Use case: logo-brand. Create a high-quality NEW BRAND EXPLORATION contact sheet for "estimador", an independent Portuguese publication and interactive data platform about economics, elections, sport and population. Three entirely distinct art-directed identities, side by side on a wide landscape board. The client rejected the previous deep-green/mint/ivory pastel palette, conventional high-contrast editorial serif type, and generic geometric arches, sun, people and pie-chart illustrations. Do NOT reuse those. Preserve ONLY the strong conceptual seed: an interval/range containing an off-centre observation point, expressing an estimate. Reinvent the execution and surrounding brand. A brand that feels culturally sharp and inviting, not finance SaaS or a maths textbook.

Each of the three vertical columns has: a large primary horizontal logo reading EXACTLY "estimador" in all lowercase with its own interval symbol; one isolated symbol and one tiny monochrome version; three small palette swatches; a bold cropped launch-page application below, with matching logo, exact headline "Portugal, em aberto." and small nav "População  Economia  Desporto  Eleições". Show distinctive use of typography and composition, not generic UI cards. Opaque flat colour backgrounds throughout. Crisp vector-like design, no transparency, gradients, shadows, glow, texture, photography or 3D. Use only column labels and the specified Portuguese text. Tiny icons are the actual brand mark, no generic decoration.

COLUMN 01, label "01 / SINAL": Contemporary Portuguese cultural magazine meets precise information design. Palette warm white #FAF8F2, electric cobalt #2945E8 and sharp tangerine #F46B3F, dark ink #202328. Custom humanist sans-serif wordmark with open apertures, slightly condensed proportions and rounded ink traps; substantial medium weight, no geometric circles, no Inter or generic corporate bold. Design the symbol as two short cobalt terminal strokes framing a floating solid tangerine point over a very short cobalt horizontal baseline; interval compact, beautifully balanced, slight asymmetry, unmistakable at small sizes. No brackets, capsule, toggle, eye or crosshair. Supporting graphic system consists of oversized cropped interval endpoints and open editorial frames that isolate fragments of type, with one coloured point acting as a punctuation mark. Bottom page bold blue typographic composition with warm white space and a surprising orange accent. Energetic, cultured, confident.

COLUMN 02, label "02 / ENTRE": Quietly expressive contemporary book publisher, warmer and more tactile in proportions but perfectly flat rendering. Palette pale pink-white #FFF4F0, oxblood #692C42, apricot #F2A477 and slate plum #473A4F. A custom low-contrast soft slab-serif wordmark with short blunt serifs and open friendly lowercase forms, medium weight; NO Didot/Bodoni/high-contrast fashion serif and no italic. Symbol a pair of short rounded oxblood vertical endcaps linked by a delicate horizontal stroke; an apricot broad translucent-looking but SOLID filled rounded interval sits between, with one oxblood point slightly left of centre. Reference concept refined into a compact elegant seal, not a UI control. Supporting graphics use broad offset colour bands, typographic annotations and generous whitespace; NO arches, suns, human icons or illustrated stairs. Bottom page warm and bookish, bold oxblood headline with apricot editorial band. Human and distinctive.

COLUMN 03, label "03 / CAMPO": Radical yet accessible digital publication with a strong typographic identity. Palette almost-white #F2F5F5, charcoal #242C34, icy blue #A7D6F1 and vivid chartreuse #D3E85B. Wordmark custom broad lowercase grotesk with unusual flat terminals and softly squared counters, precise spacing and an identifiable 't' crossbar, NOT sporty, not sci-fi, no slanted letters. The interval is integrated directly into the wordmark as a quiet extended rule beneath part of the name, with a chartreuse point near its right endpoint; standalone emblem reduces this to a short charcoal open-ended bar enclosing the point with clean small endcaps. Keep it clearly an estimate rather than a loading bar. Supporting graphics a lively editorial system of offset rectangular fields and aligned typographic fragments, designed with extreme clarity; no random geometric confetti. Bottom application large charcoal letters over icy blue fields with chartreuse punctuation, fresh and confident.

Make three genuinely different brand personalities and typefaces. The interval motif must remain subtle, legible and intentional. These are ambitious rough prototypes fit for a real brand critique.
