# Illustrated homepage directions

Three image-based composition and illustration studies, 11 September 2026. No website changes.

1. **A praça do país** — one continuous Portuguese town connects households, stadium, market and assembly. Most immersive; demands careful mobile art direction and restrained visual density.
2. **O país em aberto** — illustrated editorial layout, with distinct cutaway households, football, market and ballot/assembly scenes. Recommended starting point: easier to scan and recompose for phones while giving every section personality.
3. **Portugal, laboratório aberto** — dark forest exhibition of tactile miniature worlds, with the strongest explicit research positioning. Keep UI upright if developed; generated tilted paper labels are not a proposed control convention.

Population is a synthetic-data foundation for research and future microsimulation, with separate Explore and Investigate routes. It must not be reduced to a personal-curiosity feature.

These are art-direction prototypes, not implemented pages. Generated copy, example articles, diagrams, logo variations, data/privacy claims and depicted controls are placeholders, not validated product claims or available features. Retain the approved vector interval logo and review copy against actual data readiness. The images do not demonstrate animation or responsive behaviour; both would need implementation and verification.
