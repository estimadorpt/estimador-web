## Practical interpretation

| Keep | Improve | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm illustrated scenes, shallow depth, recognisable homes | Simplify repeated roof, balcony and plant details; preserve major forms | Photographic people, detailed isometric game worlds, sombre documentary drawings |
| Original drawn character charm | Varied three-quarter poses, expressions and meaningful interactions | Generic side-profile silhouettes, identical smiles, caricatures |
| Pine and paper brand foundation | Trial mineral blue and sage with limited clay; give colours distinct roles | Equal amounts of four pastels, yellow-on-cream muddiness, all-beige monotony |
| Architectural accents | Extract windows, thresholds, awnings and seating into a consistent small family | Copying a full scene into every header; arbitrary decorative shapes |
| Different levels of playfulness | Expressive entrances, quiet tools, occasional article accents | Decoration inside charts, perpetual movement, animated published numbers |
| Clear data and ordinary controls | Preserve source/date/uncertainty and keyboard/mobile behaviour | Building-shaped charts, invented results or nonfunctional controls |

## Scale and motion rules

Entrance artwork may be around 240–400px wide on desktop, contained rather than stretched. Research accents should be roughly 40–64px and can disappear in dense or narrow layouts. Article accents should usually be confined to related-content links or a small title accompaniment. Exports and dense results may have no illustration at all. Create simplified small assets rather than relying on shrinking detailed masters.

Reuse a small drawing vocabulary across sections, not one literal window everywhere: a window for households, a shop awning for economy, a stand entrance for football, a few assembly seats for elections. Actual interface icons remain conventional and accessible.

If motion is explored later, tie it to a meaningful user action. Keep atlas selections stable while navigating; distinguish a change of assumption from a published forecast. Respect reduced motion. These static studies demonstrate no implemented animation, simulation or responsive behaviour.

No website or production asset replacements are part of this exploration.

### What improved

- The residents have more varied faces, hair, posture and attention. They remain subordinate to the building rather than becoming brand mascots. The changes improve specificity but do not establish a uniquely recognisable character style.
- The reading card now has a small doorway beside a quiet link rather than a large scenic panel and a second dominant button.

- Supporting illustrations have been reduced to a window, awning, entrance and seats. Flags, slogans and the decorative multicolour mosaic have been removed from the supporting strip.

### How to use this direction

The application board retains the previous hero characters because that edit focused on the quieter components. Use the separate 03 image when trying the complete updated composition. Do not use the board itself as a website background or infer new product capabilities from its placeholder controls.

The small objects are **decorative accents, not navigation icons**. In particular, three seats alone do not uniquely communicate elections, and the entrance does not uniquely communicate football. Keep visible section labels and conventional interface icons. Develop a simplified vector asset family only once the art direction is chosen; shrinking these raster-board fragments is not a finished asset strategy.

Do not apply the large demonstration hero from this board to the homepage: keep the already approved asymmetric population/football hierarchy. Do not add an illustrated hero above data pages. Preserve the existing brand logo, typefaces, live-data handling and chart semantics.

The useful direction is architectural continuity at several scales, not multiplying decorations or forcing every page to have artwork. This is a measured refinement of the existing illustration, not a claim of a completely distinctive new identity.
