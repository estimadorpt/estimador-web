# Population v1.2 — the questions a parish page answers (contract, 18–19 November 2026)

Launch: Wednesday 9 December 2026, with population v1.2 and its layers. This file is the contract
between the site and the producer (estimador-microsynthesis, the v1.2 thread and the layer
threads): which new questions a parish page answers, how each response is built, and what the
bundle must carry. The site code is built against it before the national run ends, so release
day is a data sync, not a build.

The site never computes a number from cells. Every figure below is a published response in the
producer's bundle, built by the producer from the packaged files (handoff doc 206 §3). One model
run: every figure carries the lone tick (`<NoMargin>`), no ranking, no "more than", no sorting by
value, no choropleth.

## 1. The rule (Bernardo's ruling, 18 November 2026, 23:04)

Two kinds of card on a parish page:

- **Fixed per parish** (§2): an official INE table **for that parish** fixes the figure (a table
  the population or a layer was fitted or tilted to, parish by parish). The card names the table.
- **Modelled** (§2e): no parish table fixes it. These cards go in their own section, labelled
  "Modelado" / "Modelled", with the same lone tick, and each source line says what anchors it:
  income per município; birthplace per parish on one share (PT nationals born abroad);
  residence in 2019 and school attendance by no table at any level (census thread, doc 240 §7).
  The card asked for "anchored at município level"; that is true of income only, so the section
  label says "modelled" and the source lines carry the detail. Bernardo chose this on the decision card ("Show them, labelled") over the
  download-only recommendation.

Everything else is in the download and listed by name on `/populacao/dados`.

## 2. Parish cards at launch (fixed by a parish table)

Statements are the card titles (`RECIPE_COPY[*].short`, site copy). "Universe" is the query's
filter set. Recipe = the manifest key without `portrait_` (the sync reads `portrait_<recipe>`).
Every query: `geography_scope: all_freguesias`, `measure: share`, `normalization: grand_total`,
`minimum_cell: 0`, surfaces `["portrait"]` (never `game`: the misteriosa deck and its calendar do
not change).

### 2a. People (core population, fitted tables)

| recipe | statement (pt / en) | unit · dimension | universe (filters) | what fixes it per parish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `nationality` | Nacionalidade / Nationality | person · `nationality_group` | everyone | `p_nat`, one of the 12 fitted person tables |
| `marital` | Estado civil / Marital status | person · `marital_status_code` | persons with a marital status (the fitted table's universe: producer to name the filter) | `p_marital` (INE 12868), fitted |
| `income_source` | Principal meio de vida / Main source of livelihood | person · `income_source_code` | persons with a value (the fitted table's universe) | `p_income` (MEIOVIDA), fitted |

### 2b. Work (v1.2 fits the work tables per parish after the household choice, DECISIONS §64)

| recipe | statement (pt / en) | unit · dimension | universe | what fixes it per parish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sitprof` | Situação na profissão / Status in employment | person · `sitprof_code` | employed, 15+ (see universes below) | `p_sitprof`, fitted |
| `occupation` | Profissão / Occupation | person · `occupation_major` | employed (see below) | INE 12306 (sex × CPP), fitted in v1.2 (`w_occupation`) |
| `industry` | Atividade económica / Industry | person · `industry_section` | employed (see below) | INE 12370 (sex × CAE section), fitted in v1.2 (`w_industry`) |
| `work_place` | Onde trabalham ou estudam / Where people work or study | person · `work_location_type` | the fitted table's universe (12369: employed and students) | INE 12369, fitted in v1.2 (`w_worklocation`) |
| `transport` | Como vão para o trabalho ou a escola / How people get to work or school | person · `transport_mode` | the fitted table's universe (12371) | INE 12371, fitted in v1.2 (`w_transport`) |

**Universes, as the v1.2 thread confirmed (18 Nov, 23:07 UTC).** In v1.2 every person table is
resident: collective-quarters residents keep their seed record's attributes, work included
(DECISIONS §65, C2), so ask 28 no longer holds and no card excludes them.
- sitprof: `employment_status_code = "11"`, aged 15+, `sitprof_code` not null.
- occupation (12306), industry (12370): `employment_status_code = "11"`, the card's column not null.
- work_place (12369): employed and students, all ages, `work_location_type` not null.
- transport (12371): the same, with `work_location_type` in 2–5 and `transport_mode` not null.
- marital (12868): `age_12grp` and `marital_status_code` not null. income_source: 15+, not null.
- Homes: `is_institutional = 0`, tenure not null.

`household_type`: packaging does not rebuild it today; v1.1 and the v1.2 bed trees rewrite 0
households, so v1.2 should be clean. The producer runs `build-public-responses` plus a
verify-release cross-check after the run; the site drops its caveat once the release passes it.
At the v1.2 bump the site also drops "valores atribuídos depois do ajuste" from the education and
employment source lines (`INSTITUTIONAL_AFTER_FIT`), since C2 writes those residents in the
generator.

### 2d. Origin and schooling (census facts layer, chain-with-inputs "C+", tilted to parish tables)

| recipe | statement (pt / en) | unit · dimension | universe | what fixes it per parish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lived_abroad` | Pessoas que já viveram no estrangeiro / People who have lived abroad | person · `lived_abroad` | everyone | INE 12297 (= BGRI), parish × sex, tilted |
| `literate` | Pessoas que não sabem ler nem escrever / People who cannot read or write | person · `literate` | aged 10+ (`age_5y` in 10–14 … 90+) | INE 12274, parish × sex, tilted |

**The five draws.** The sidecar carries `_d1` … `_d5`. A response's share is the **mean over the
five draws** (person-draws in the category ÷ person-draws in the universe), so it equals the
tilted expectation and is reproducible from the sidecar. The source line says "média de cinco
sorteios". **Producer: confirm, or say which draw you publish.**

### 2e. Modelled (own section, labelled)

| recipe | statement (pt / en) | unit · dimension | universe | what anchors it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `income` | Rendimento bruto declarado / Gross declared income | person · `income_band` (new derived column, bands set by the producer: e.g. none, under 5 000 €, 5–10, 10–20, 20–40, 40 000 € or more a year) | persons aged 18+ (producer to confirm the universe E14 covers) | IRS 2021 by município and SS tables (E14 ecological fit); the parish split is the model's |
| `birthplace` | Onde nasceram / Where people were born | person · `birthplace` (1 this parish, 2 another parish of the município, 3 another município, 4 abroad) | everyone | 12296 fixes PT nationals born abroad per parish; the split of those born in Portugal is the model's |
| `residence_2019` | Onde viviam em 2019 / Where people lived in 2019 | person · `residence_2019` (1 not yet born … 6 abroad) | everyone | none at any level (12312 is a check, not a target) |
| `school_attendance` | Frequência de ensino / School attendance | person · `school_attendance` (1 attending, 2 attended, 3 never) | everyone | none at any level; the record (age, education) carries it |

Census-facts shares are means over the five draws, as in §2d. Income: one draw (E14 is a single
column), its band per person.

## 4. What the bundle must carry (producer asks)

1. **Manifest** (`config/public/launch_queries.json`, schema v1, contract 1.0): one
   `portrait_<recipe>` query per row of §2, in the order of §2 after the eight existing ones,
   with `field_provenance` for every new dimension and filter field (`census_calibrated` for the
   fitted and tilted ones; the census-facts fields may be a new class only if `contract.ts` is
   told: say so before you write it).
2. **Responses** for every parish (3,092) and every new recipe, built from the packaged files
   (core persons/households, the census-facts sidecar for §2d), categories in the code order of
   the label map, a category with no one in it present as 0 (as since v1.0.1). National
   responses: not needed at launch.
3. **Permalink buckets** (`responses_v1.2.x/`) for the new query ids, as now.
4. **`label_maps` in `metadata.json`** for every new dimension (pt-PT labels at least) and for
   the three the site still supplies (`activity_sector_code`, `education_level_coarse5`, `nuts2`).
5. **Dictionary rows** (`metadata.json` columns) for the sidecars: each column's description,
   universe, anchor or "none", informativeness, and **licence per file**: population and census
   facts CC BY-NC 4.0 with INE's CC BY 4.0 credit; income CC BY-NC 4.0; vote columns from EEP
   CC BY-NC; attitude columns from ESS CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Each file's name, size and SHA-256 in
   `release.json`'s package list (the site tests the sizes it prints against it).
6. **`household_type`** rebuilt from the packaged `hh_type_top` (ask 25), so the site can drop its
   caveat; say in the handoff whether it is.
7. **Permalinks across releases** (ask 31): an old id → new id map in the bundle, or stable ids
   for unchanged queries.
8. **The game deck**: unchanged calendar (`POPULATION_GAME_EPOCH` 2026-10-06, day 0 = 030857).
9. **Model card and errata** text for the new questions' limitations, which the site quotes.

## 6. How the site builds it (estimador-web)

- `PortraitRecipe` = the eight core recipes + the optional layer recipes of §2 (provenance `fitted`, `tilted` or `modelled`). A card renders
  only when the parish file carries its response; with v1.0.3 data nothing changes.
- Each card: `RECIPE_COPY` (statement, population, anchor), `VALUES` (labels in code order),
  `DIMENSION_LABEL`, a provenance class (`fitted` | `tilted` | `derived`) and its source line
  naming the table, a table twin, the lone tick, the place line.
- Sections on the page: Pessoas, Origem, Trabalho, Casas e famílias, then "Modelado" (rail and "Nesta página").
- `/populacao/dados`: the files list grows from config (each file with its licence), the
  dictionary reads the new rows.
- Fixture: `src/lib/population/fixtures/v1.2-parish.json` (synthetic values, `data_status:
  "fixture"`, never served) drives the tests until the real bundle exists.
