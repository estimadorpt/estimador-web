## Our mission

Each section answers a question about Portugal with data and an explained model; [the list below](#o-que-esta-publicado) says what is published today and in what state.

We believe that understanding the country takes more than commentary: it takes numbers with a date, a source and a stated uncertainty, and models explained well enough to be checked.

## Our approach

Each section uses the method its question calls for: Bayesian statistical models for Liga Portugal and the elections, and a synthetic population fitted to the 2021 Census tables for the population. In all of them, the numbers state their date, their source and how uncertain they are, or say that no margin of error was calculated.

**Principles:**

- **Transparency:** open methodology, with documentation of the models, the data and the assumptions.
- **Independence:** no external funding and no editorial pressure.
- **Accuracy:** we say how each result is evaluated and claim only what is published. The Liga Portugal model is checked against actual results and bookmakers' closing odds in [Model vs market](/en/desporto/liga/modelo/); how we measure the synthetic population's quality is on [Quality](/en/populacao/qualidade/), and each parish's tier (A, B or C) is on the parish's own page. An evaluation of the election forecasts against the results is not published.
- **Accessibility:** numbers and probabilities presented so that any reader can follow them.

---

## Limitations and caveats

Probabilistic forecasting is uncertain by nature, which is why we present probabilities, not certainties. A forecast of 70% also means a 30% chance of the opposite. The models account for several sources of uncertainty, but there are always unpredictable factors.

The synthetic population is not a forecast: it is people and households generated to reproduce the Census tables, in a single run with no margin of error calculated. Read its numbers as an estimate of the 2021 picture, not as a count of real people.

## Can I reuse the figures and charts?

Yes, with attribution. The figures, tables and charts that estimador.pt's models produce (the Liga Portugal forecasts, the archived election forecasts and the synthetic population) may be reused under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): name estimador.pt as the source and link to the page they come from.

The licence does not cover the third-party data the site shows beside those figures: polls, official results, bookmakers' odds, players' market values and match statistics stay under the terms of their sources, which each page names. Nor does it cover the estimador.pt name, logo and mark.

To cite a page: estimador.pt, "page title", page address, accessed (date). The synthetic population has its own citation, with the release number, under [Open data](/en/populacao/dados/#citar), and every parish page ends with a "How to cite" block; the Liga Portugal files and their terms are on [Liga Portugal data](/en/desporto/liga/dados/).
