## What it is

<NoMargin locale="en">Generated people and households, not real people, families, or addresses.</NoMargin>

estimador.pt’s synthetic population is a set of people and households generated, one by one, for each of the 3,092 parishes of the 2021 Census (CAOP 2021). Every parish answers the same questions with its own numbers and its own measured quality tier.

## Why it is needed

INE publishes, for each parish, tables that count one thing at a time: how many people of each age, how many households of each size. They do not say how those things combine inside homes, such as how many people aged 65 or over live alone in a given parish. The [methodology](/en/populacao/metodologia) shows the problem with an invented parish of 10 homes and 23 people, and the five steps that generate the population.

## What is new in v1.2

Every parish page answers new questions. Some are fixed, parish by parish, by an INE table for that parish that the population was fitted to:

- **Work:** status in employment, occupation, industry, where people work or study and how they get there (INE tables 12306, 12370, 12369 and 12371, fitted per parish for the first time).
- **Home:** whether it is owned or rented, how many rooms it has, when the building was built and whether a wheelchair can get in.
- **Origin and schooling:** who has lived abroad and who cannot read or write (tables 12297 and 12274), as a mean of five draws.

Others are the model's and sit in a section of their own, “Modelled”, with what anchors them: declared income (anchored on the IRS tables by município), where people were born, where they lived in 2019 and school attendance. Vote and the 20 attitudes stay in the download, labelled by how much they say about each person.
